MindSpace

A private place for students to reflect, understand their feelings, and connect with peers in a healthy way.

Premium student wellness and self-reflection platform

What it is

MindSpace is a mobile wellness app that complements campus advisory programs. Students log private voice or text reflections, get AI insights on tone and themes, and connect with peers on their own terms. The result: stronger emotional awareness, daily self-care habits, and positive friendships.

Scope and AI disclaimer

MindSpace is a self-reflection and peer connection tool only. It does not give medical advice, diagnosis, or clinical therapy. The AI analyzes and visualizes emotional tone and recurring themes from what the student provides. It is built to complement, never replace, school counseling departments, pastoral advisory teams, and medical professionals.
